# Consolidated source specification represented by the default config

The default project captures the current housing brief as an editable baseline:

- two physical parts: lower base and upper hinged lid;
- general wall thickness 2 mm;
- orthographic front, top and side documentation for each part and the assembly;
- external width 79 mm;
- total height 40 mm;
- base height 25 mm;
- flat lid section approximately 80 mm in the depth direction;
- 2 mm vertical section at the lower end of the lid walls;
- 45 degree front hinge-side transition;
- hinge opening preview above 190 degrees;
- Raspberry Pi 5 reference board 85 x 56 mm;
- four 3 mm high, Ø6 mm standoffs with Ø1 mm pilot holes for each mounting option;
- mounting option A: 2 mm from the internal front wall and 1 mm from the internal right wall;
- mounting option B: anchored 7.5 mm from the internal right wall, with the requested 10.5 mm left value retained as a validation target;
- six camera bosses and four additional Ø8/Ø2 lid bosses;
- top datum of auxiliary lid bosses at 14 mm from the upper base mating plane (Datum A);
- layered DXF documentation and separate PDF sheets for base, lid and assembly.

## Parameters not proven by the source material

The source images and correspondence do not fully define every connector cut-out, the precise hinge pin method, component envelopes, all fillets, ventilation, screw standards or final production tolerances. Those items remain explicit configurable assumptions and are flagged for prototype verification.
